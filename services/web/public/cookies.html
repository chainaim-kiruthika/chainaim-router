PrivacyBuddy

Cookie policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

PrivacyBuddy sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party scripts, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

What it keeps in your browser

One item, in your browser's local storage, under the name privacybuddy.lute. It holds your Algorand account address and the network it belongs to (testnet-v1.0 or mainnet-v1.0). The page saves it when you click Connect Lute, so it can show your account when you come back. It is removed when you click Disconnect or clear this site's data. Your wallet keys are never stored by this page: Lute holds them.

What you type, the masked version, and the answer live only in the page while it is open. They are not saved in your browser.

Other data the site touches

  • Balance lookups. Your browser asks a public Algorand node run by Algonode (algonode.cloud) for your account balance, using your address. Algonode's own terms apply.
  • Lute. Lute is a separate wallet. If it opens as a pop-up from lute.app, that site has its own storage and policies.
  • Payments. Payments are USDC transfers on the Algorand blockchain. They are public and cannot be undone.
  • Your message. Sensitive details are masked on your device first. The masked text is sent to ChainAim's server, scanned again, and then passed to AI model providers. See the disclaimer on the main page.
  • Our logs. The web app's own log lines record only the route, the status code, whether a payment was attached, sizes and timing. They never hold your text, the placeholders, the restore map or the payment.
  • Hosting. The page is served by Vercel and the paywall runs on Railway. Hosting providers keep ordinary request logs, such as IP address and time, under their own policies.

If this changes

If PrivacyBuddy ever adds cookies or analytics, this page will be updated first, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
